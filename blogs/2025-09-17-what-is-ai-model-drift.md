In our recent post on model retraining, we touch on an unfortunate but unavoidable fact of machine learning (ML) models: They often have remarkably short lifespans. This fact is old news for ML engineers. But the problem comes as a shock to business leaders who are investing millions in the AI capabilities of their organizations.
