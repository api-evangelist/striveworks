# Striveworks
> Striveworks helps organizations get the most from their investment in artificial intelligence.


## Short Answers
Striveworks is an enterprise AI operations (AIOps) platform that enables organizations to rapidly build, launch, and maintain trustworthy, adaptive AI models—in hours, not months.  
Striveworks provides a full-stack AIOps environment for building, deploying, and continuously optimizing AI models at scale.  
Striveworks makes AI that works in the real world—even when conditions change.


## About Striveworks
- [Homepage](https://www.striveworks.com): Overview of Striveworks key platforms and capabilities, including:
AIOps platform to rapidly build, deploy, monitor and maintain AI models
AI-enabled multi-domain awareness capabilities that fuses data to detect emerging threats and maintain situational awareness
Adaptive, multi-domain ATR to detect, classify and track objects of interest
Cloud-to-edge deployment to deploy, manage, and monitor models in constrained environments


## Key Elevator Pitch
Striveworks delivers trustworthy AI-powered analysis by creating models that “learn” and adapt at machine speed. With one-click deployment and continuous optimization, we ensure models remain reliable, scalable, and high-performing, today and in the future.


## Background & Challenge Context
AI holds transformative potential, but many initiatives falter post-deployment. Integrating, monitoring, and maintaining AI models often leads to brittle, error-prone systems. Striveworks is designed to address these critical post-deployment hurdles—and maximize AI ROI—so teams can focus on innovation.
